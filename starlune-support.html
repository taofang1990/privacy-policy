Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Your sign, settings, consent choice, and saved readings stay on your device.

How does the Oracle handle my data?

The app shows the exact fields, recipient, purpose, and processing location before sending anything. No AI request is made until you actively agree. You can withdraw consent at You > AI data consent.

How do I manage Starlune Plus?

Open Settings on your device, tap your Apple Account, then Subscriptions. You can also use Restore Purchases on the Starlune paywall.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next renewal.

Why does the Oracle say AI is not configured?

That build cannot reach the AI gateway. Daily readings, the zodiac library, reminders, and relationship tools remain available offline.

Is Starlune medical or financial advice?

No. Starlune is a reflective astrology experience and does not provide medical, legal, financial, or mental-health advice.

How do I delete saved readings?

Open You > Reading Library, swipe left on a reading, and choose Delete. Uninstalling the app also removes local app data.

常见问题

需要注册账号吗？

不需要。星座、设置、授权选择和收藏解读都保存在你的设备中。

星语如何处理我的数据？

发送前，应用会展示具体字段、接收方、用途与处理地区。只有你主动同意后才会发送 AI 请求。可在“我的 > AI 数据授权”中撤回。

如何管理 Starlune Plus？

打开设备“设置”，进入 Apple 账户，再进入“订阅”。也可以在星澜订阅页使用“恢复购买”。

如何取消订阅？

订阅由 Apple 管理。请在当前周期结束前至少 24 小时取消，以避免下一次续期。

为什么星语显示 AI 尚未配置？

该构建暂时无法连接 AI 网关。每日解读、星座图鉴、提醒和关系工具仍可离线使用。

星澜是否提供医疗或财务建议？

不提供。星澜是反思式占星体验，不构成医疗、法律、财务或心理健康建议。

如何删除收藏解读？

进入“我的 > 解读收藏”，向左滑动解读并选择删除。卸载应用也会移除本地应用数据。
